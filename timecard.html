One-time setup (about 5 minutes) — click to see the steps
  1. Open the tool from its web address (not a saved file) — direct sending needs a real website. Your current address:
  2. Go to portal.azure.com → sign in with your Microsoft account → search App registrations → New registration.
  3. Name it Timecard. Under supported account types pick “Accounts in any organizational directory and personal Microsoft accounts”.
  4. Under Redirect URI choose Single-page application (SPA) and paste your address from step 1.
  5. Press Register, copy the Application (client) ID shown at the top, and paste it in the box above.
  6. Click Sign in to Microsoft here and approve. Done — from now on the three send buttons put the email straight into your Outlook with the picture/table/PDF already attached. If your work account is blocked by IT, sign in with your personal Hotmail instead.
How sending works: every button silently creates the complete email — To, CCs, subject, your message on top and the timecard already inside it (📷 picture / 📝 colored table / 📄 .pdf attached). No dialogs, nothing to attach, no Ctrl+V. Open the created file and it appears in Outlook as a ready draft — press Send.
⚡ Make it 100% automatic — do this once: after the first click, right-click the file in the browser's download bar → “Always open files of this type”. From then on every click makes the finished email pop open by itself, attached and addressed — you only press Send.
🧠 Drop-down memory: every value you type in the sheet is remembered and offered as a drop-down next time. To forget a value, open the drop-down and click the ✕ next to it.
The backup file holds everything: settings, drop-down memory and all saved weeks. Import it on another computer to move the tool.
